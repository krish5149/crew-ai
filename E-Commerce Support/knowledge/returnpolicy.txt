Return and Refund Policy

At Your Brand, we stand behind the quality of our products and want you to shop with confidence. If you're not completely satisfied with your purchase, we're here to help.

1. Return Window

You have 30 days from the date your order is delivered to request a return. Unfortunately, returns requested after 30 days cannot be accepted.

2. Return Eligibility

To qualify for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

Unused and unworn
In their original condition
Include all original packaging, accessories, and tags
Free from damage caused by misuse or improper handling

We reserve the right to reject returns that do not meet these requirements.

3. How to Start a Return

To initiate a return:

Visit yourstore.com/returns and enter your order number and email address, or
Email us at support@yourstore.com

Once your request is reviewed, we will provide a prepaid return shipping label or detailed return instructions within 1-2 business days.

4. Return Shipping Costs

We offer free return shipping for all eligible returns. If you received a defective, damaged, or incorrect item, return shipping will be covered entirely by us.

5. Refund Process

After we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund.

Approved refunds will be issued to your original payment method within 5-10 business days. Depending on your financial institution, additional processing time may be required before the funds appear in your account.

6. Exchanges

Need a different size, color, or replacement item?

Simply start an exchange request through yourstore.com/returns. Once we receive and inspect the original item, we will ship the replacement as quickly as possible, subject to product availability.

7. Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Items

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or you receive the wrong item, please contact us within 7 days of delivery.

To help us resolve the issue quickly, include:

Your order number
A brief description of the issue
Clear photos of the product and packaging

We will replace the item or provide a full refund at no additional cost to you.

8. Non-Returnable Items

The following items are considered final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged:

Gift cards
Personalized or custom-made products
Clearance and final sale items
Opened consumable products
Personal care or hygiene items
Downloadable digital products
9. Restocking Fees

We do not charge restocking fees on eligible returns.

10. Order Cancellations

Orders may be canceled within 12 hours of placement or before shipment, whichever comes first. Once an order has been shipped, it cannot be canceled and must be returned according to this policy.

11. Late or Missing Refunds

If you have not received your refund after the stated processing time:

Check your bank account again.
Contact your credit card company or payment provider.
Contact your bank, as processing times may vary.

If you have completed these steps and still have not received your refund, please contact us.

12. Contact Us

If you have any questions about returns, refunds, or exchanges, we're happy to help.
